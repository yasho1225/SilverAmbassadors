# Silver Ambassadors

Student organization at Alpharetta High School connecting students with senior citizens in Alpharetta through weekly volunteering.

## Audience
Prospective and active student members, parents, school community, senior participants.

## Primary action
Submit volunteer hours via the official Google Form; secondary: find Sunday events.

## Voice
Clear, warm, trustworthy. No invented stats, partnerships, or contacts.

## Constraints
- Brand name: Silver Ambassadors (alias Silver Raiders on some school materials)
- Mission and activity copy are fixed (see `src/lib/content.ts`)
- Neutral silver/charcoal world; no purple SaaS, no cream-terracotta costume
- Placeholders until logo, photos, calendar URL, and contacts are provided
